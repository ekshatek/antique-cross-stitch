## About the Collection

This collection is an online repository of antique cross stitch patterns I hope to stitch myself someday. The hope is that by having all items in one place, searching will be streamlined.

All materials in this collection were sourced from antiquepatternlibrary.org and are licensed under the Creative Commons.
